The Tools

I need your help: I can give you the tools, but I don't know how to convince people to use them. Help me, commies, you're my only hope.

  • Firefox / Brave - Internet browsers
    • Chrome is blocking ad blockers, and has tracking
    • Firefox + uBlock Origin = ad free Youtube!
  • OpenStreetMap, OsmAnd, Mapillary - Google Maps / Google Street View alternatives
    • Huge ecosystem, all community supported
    • Protection from Google pushing you to its favorite restaurants
    • Frequently used in disaster relief - see: Rebecca Solnit
    • Very fun and addicting
  • Pixelfed - Instagram alternative
    • You aren't a human, you're a meat robot AI agent that generates photo content to train Instagram AI
    • Join now! pixelfed.tw
  • Signal - Messaging app
    • Moxie Marlinspike - Anarchist
    • E2E Encrypted
    • Most downloaded app in HK after national security law
    • Important to hide your communication by default - you don't know if you're breaking the law, and governments can change.
  • Grayjay - Youtube client
    • Youtube on your phone with no ads, background play, downloads etc
  • Briar - Encrypted device to device communication
    • Usable if internet goes down
    • Or if cell towers are closed around protests
  • Meshtastic - Mesh network communication
    • Similar to Briar, but each device can help messages get across the city
    • See also: Reticulum. Meshtastic but for any kind of internet traffic
  • Linux - Constellation of operating systems
    • Frees you from Apple and Windows surveillance
    • Lets you install whatever software you want - including software Apple doesn't want you to install
  • Libreoffice - Document editor
    • Trump removed Microsoft Office access to people abroad that annoyed him
  • Nextcloud - Google Drive alternative
  • Mastodon + Bluesky - Twitter alternatives
    • Lets you choose your own algorithm
    • Protects you from psychological attack via ads, targeted content, selective censorship
  • Peertube - Youtube alternative
    • Avoid Youtube censorship
    • Youtube algorithm is cursed: see Elsagate
  • Matrix / Element - Slack / Discord alternative
  • Hackfoldr - Notion alternative (markdown editing)

  • Tor - Encrypted Internet Access
    • Hides traffic from ISPs and governments
    • Used during Tunisian revolution
