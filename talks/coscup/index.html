SaaS

Most companies have mulitiple SaaS they onboard employees to. These have a cost-per-employee.

  • Notion: Wiki. 10$/person
  • Salesforce: CRM. 150$/person
  • Quickbooks: Finance. 20$/person
  • Docusign: Signature. 40$/person
  • Google Docs: Documents, file sharing, calendar, email. 10$/person
  • Onepass or similar: Password and secret management. 10$/person
  • Heroku or similar: Platform deployment. 10$/person
  • Linear: Project management. 20$/person

Self Hosting

Self Hosting means that, instead of paying a monthly SaaS bill for access to a service, you deploy and manage a service yourself.

Here's the services 508.dev uses.

508.dev

  • Outline: Wiki
  • EspoCRM: CRM
  • ERPNext: Finance / ERP / Invoicing / Timesheets
  • OpenProject: Project Management
  • Docuseal: Document Signing
  • Cal.com: Calendar and meeting scheduling
  • Vaultwarden: Password and secret sharing
  • Nextcloud: Filesharing, documents, calendar, email client
  • Coolify: Platform deployment
